免疫細胞 Immune Cells

這是什麼What it is

免疫細胞是人體的「防禦部隊」，會在全身巡邏，辨識並清除入侵的病菌， 以及不正常、老化或癌變的細胞。

Immune cells are the body's defense force — patrolling the body to identify and clear invading pathogens and abnormal, aging, or cancerous cells.

特點Key features
  • 表面受體能即時偵測病菌、病毒與異常細胞Surface receptors detect pathogens, viruses, and abnormal cells in real time
  • NK 細胞：不需事先辨認，直接攻擊受感染或異常細胞NK cells: attack infected or abnormal cells directly, without prior recognition
  • CIK 細胞：實驗室培養，增殖與毒殺能力強CIK cells: lab-cultured, with strong proliferation and cytotoxic ability
已知功能與應用方向Known roles & applications
  • 是人體對抗病原、監控異常細胞的自然防線The body's natural defense against pathogens and abnormal cells
  • NK、CIK 細胞在腫瘤免疫等領域受到廣泛研究NK and CIK cells are widely studied in tumor immunology
  • 為細胞免疫療法研究的重要基礎A foundation for research into cellular immunotherapy
我們的優勢Our advantages
  • 建立標準化、可重複的免疫細胞製備流程Standardized, reproducible immune cell preparation processes
  • 提供 CIK、NK 等免疫細胞的培養與品質管理Culture and quality control of immune cells including CIK and NK

幹細胞 Stem Cells

這是什麼What it is

幹細胞是身體的「原始細胞」，能自我複製，也能分化成多種功能細胞， 是再生醫學的重要基礎。

Stem cells are the body's original cells — they can self-renew and differentiate into many functional cell types, forming a cornerstone of regenerative medicine.

特點Key features
  • 具「自我複製」與「分化成多種細胞」的能力Able to self-renew and differentiate into many cell types
  • 常見來源：臍帶（不影響母嬰）、脂肪組織Common sources: umbilical cord (no impact on mother or baby), adipose tissue
  • 是再生醫學與細胞應用的重要基礎A foundation for regenerative medicine and cell applications
已知功能與應用方向Known roles & applications
  • 具組織修復與再生的潛力，是再生醫學研究核心Potential for tissue repair and regeneration; central to regenerative medicine research
  • 間質幹細胞具免疫調節、分泌生長因子等特性Mesenchymal stem cells show immunomodulatory and growth-factor-secreting properties
  • 廣泛應用於組織工程與細胞治療研究Widely studied in tissue engineering and cell therapy
我們的優勢Our advantages
  • 專注於分離、組織培養與嚴謹品管Focused on isolation, tissue culture, and rigorous quality control
  • 建立穩定且可重複的細胞技術平台A stable, reproducible cell technology platform

外泌體 Exosomes

這是什麼What it is

外泌體是細胞釋放出來的「奈米級小包裹」，攜帶蛋白質、核酸等訊息分子， 是細胞之間互相傳遞訊號的媒介。

Exosomes are nano-sized packages released by cells, carrying proteins, nucleic acids, and other signaling molecules — a medium for communication between cells.

特點Key features
  • 表面帶有 CD9、CD63、CD81 等標記蛋白Carry surface markers such as CD9, CD63, CD81
  • 內含蛋白質、microRNA 等訊息分子Enclose signaling molecules such as proteins and microRNA
  • 體積約 50–200 奈米（nm），極為微小About 50–200 nanometers (nm) in size — extremely small
  • 穩定性佳，適合保存與應用Stable — well suited to storage and application
已知功能與應用方向Known roles & applications
  • 參與細胞間訊息傳遞，影響組織修復與發炎等過程Mediate cell-to-cell signaling, influencing tissue repair and inflammation
  • 攜帶生長因子與 RNA，被研究用於再生醫學與皮膚保養Carry growth factors and RNA; studied for regenerative medicine and skincare
  • 於醫美、保健與動物保健領域具發展潛力Promising in aesthetic medicine, health, and animal care
我們的優勢Our advantages
  • 建立外泌體的分離與生產技術Established exosome isolation and production technologies
  • 發展人用與動物用（含美妝、保健）等多元應用Diverse applications for human and animal use, including cosmetics and health
